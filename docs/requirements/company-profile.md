Hericilla Support Services Limited is a complete Supply Chain Services provider that specialises in total logistics and procurement. ## Management Team

### Ogundipe Paul — Managing Director
Chartered Accountant with over twenty-five years of extensive working experience in Finance, auditing, manufacturing, trading, oil & gas industries, supply chain management and global logistics. Served as Internal Auditor for a multinational company, Senior Accountant, Financial Controller and Assets, Agency, Commercial Manager and Supply Chain Group Lead for AOS Orwell Limited before establishing Hericilla Support Services Ltd. Graduate of Accounting, MBA (Ladoke Akintola University of Technology), MSc Corporate Governance (Leeds Metropolitan University, UK). Fellow of ICAO-N (ICAN) and Nigerian Institute of Management (NIM).

### Bright Efekoro — Executive Director
Over twenty-two years of hands-on global logistics management experience. Worked in blue-chip logistics companies in Nigeria including Chellarams PLC, IAL Nigeria Limited and as Logistics Manager at Schlumberger Nigeria Limited. Experience spans complete supply chain services as they relate to the Oil and Gas business in Nigeria. Consults for major oil & gas downstream operators in Nigeria.

### Ogundipe Kikelomo — Director
Graduate of Management Science. Over ten years' experience at the higher cadre of the civil service commission before coming into Logistics with expertise in international removals business. Presently an international business network planner; coordinates the forecast planning of the company.

### Joan Okere — Operations Manager
Graduate of History and International Relations, University of Lagos, with twelve years' work experience in logistics. Worked as Transport Manager in heavy-duty land trucking companies in Nigeria. Currently manages the Trucking/Transport operations of the company.
